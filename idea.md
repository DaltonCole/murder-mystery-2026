# Murder Mystery 2026

## Theme - (Era??)

* Cloths: Fluffy dresses and tux
* Masquerade - Think Bridgerton season 4

## Concept

* Three teams - similar to Feed the Kraken

### Fractions

* There will be three fractions:
    1) Aristocrat
    2) Revolutionary
    3) Cultist

* Those who come late will be a fourth category: servant
    * This is because they came late so they can't be a main role

#### The Fractions

##### Aristocrat

###### Win Condition

* King/Queen is alive at the end of the night and is not a cultist

###### Characters

* King/Queen:
    * Count: 1
    * Goal: Survive the night
    * Starting knowledge: None
    * Ability: None
* Prince/Princess:
    * Count: 1
    * Goal: Protect the King/Queen
    * Starting knowledge:
    * Ability: None
* Priest/Priestess:
    * Count: 1
    * Goal: Protect the King/Queen
    * Starting knowledge: None
    * Ability: Able to protect one person from.... Something
        * The Priest/Priestess does not know what they are protecting from, but they are protecting one person from the cult leader conversion.
            * The cult leader may not select this person to join their cult during this cult selection round. The cult leader selects someone else to convert.

##### Cultist

###### Characters

* Cult Leader:
    * Count: 1
    * Goal: Convert the King/Queen to the cult AND have the king/queen alive at the end of the night
    * Starting knowledge: None
* Cultist:
    * Count: 0
    * Goal: Help the cult leader
    * Starting knowledge:
        * Knows who their fellow cultists are

###### Game Play

* During set times within the game, the cult leader recruits a cultist to their cause.

#### Character Determination

* During character creation, have every person rate 1-10 about how involved they would like to be in the game.
    * Anyone 5 and under in involvement cannot be a major character (unless if there is not enough interest in being a major character). Above 5, give probability on an scale like:
        * 6 involvement = 1 ticket
        * 7 involvement = 5 tickets
        * 8 involvement = 20 tickets
        * 9 involvement = 50 tickets
        * 10 involvement = 100 tickets
        * i.e., those who would like to be really involved are much more likely to be a main major

### Game play

* Play time: 2 hours
* There will be 6 rounds along with two special rounds
    * Each round lasts 15 minutes
    * The two special rounds are the first and last rounds
